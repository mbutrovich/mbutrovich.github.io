I am a distributed database engineer at [Apple](https://www.apple.com), building lakehouse systems for the Data Platform team. I currently focus on [Apache DataFusion Comet](https://datafusion.apache.org/comet/), an accelerator for [Apache Spark](https://spark.apache.org). This work also has me contributing to [Apache Arrow](https://arrow.apache.org), [Apache DataFusion](https://datafusion.apache.org), [Apache Iceberg](https://iceberg.apache.org), and [Apache Parquet](https://parquet.apache.org), and I serve on the [Project Management Committee (PMC)](https://www.apache.org/foundation/governance/pmcs.html) for Apache DataFusion.

I earned my Ph.D. in Computer Science at [Carnegie Mellon University](https://www.cmu.edu) (CMU), where I researched database management systems (DBMSs) with the [CMU Database Group](https://db.cs.cmu.edu), advised by [Andy Pavlo](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~pavlo/). My work explored operating system and DBMS co-design via safe kernel extension mechanisms like [eBPF](https://ebpf.io/what-is-ebpf/).

Before my doctorate I completed an M.Sc. in Computer Science, also at CMU. Earlier, I spent over eight years at [Western Digital](https://www.westerndigital.com) validating storage-device firmware while earning a B.Sc. in Computer Science & Engineering at [UC Irvine](https://uci.edu) and [Irvine Valley College](https://www.ivc.edu).
